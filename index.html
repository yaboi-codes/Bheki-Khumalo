About

A Career at the Intersection of Power & Purpose

Bheki Khumalo is Vice President of Government Relations at Anglo American South Africa — a role that draws on nearly four decades of experience spanning grassroots civic leadership, presidential communications, multinational corporations, and public sector governance.

Bheki's public life began in local government, where he served as a Councillor on the Kyalami Metropolitan Council and the Tembisa–Kempton Park Municipal Council from 1985 to 1998, rising to Deputy Chairman of EXCO and chairing the municipality's Electricity Sub-Committee.

His corporate career began in 1996 at Energy Measurements Limited in Isando — a joint venture between Siemens and Spescom operating within Siemens Switchgear — where he worked as a Sales Support Specialist focused on prepaid electricity meters. He went on to serve as Education Spokesperson for Ministers Sibusiso Bengu and Kader Asmal (1998–2001), before being appointed Spokesperson for President Thabo Mbeki (2001–2005).

In 2005, Bheki returned to the private sector as Executive Director of Corporate Affairs and Major Events at Siemens South Africa. From 2011 to 2016, he served as Vice President of Corporate Affairs, BEE, and Government Relations at Sasol, sitting on the Global Group Executive Committee (GEC) and on the boards of several Sasol subsidiaries — including gas, synfuels, mining, ChemCity, oil, Infrachem, Mafikeng IDZ, and Anglo American Inyosi. Since 2016, he has led Government Relations for Anglo American South Africa as Vice President.

His board service has also spanned the SABC, South African Tourism, and the North West Development Corporation and Mafikeng Industrial Development Zone (MIDZ) — which he chaired from 2007 to 2016 — as well as the Black Management Forum. He currently serves on the board of the National Business Initiative (NBI).

Bheki holds a BA, BA Honours, and Higher Diploma in Education from the University of Fort Hare, and has completed advanced executive education through the University of Cambridge, the University of Queensland, and the Wits Business School.
